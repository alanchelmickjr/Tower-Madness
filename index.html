🏢 TOWER MADNESS 🏢

Elevator Operator

🤖 The Story: You're the elevator operator at Frontier Tower, caught between two worlds:

⬆️ Floor 4: The Good Robot Lab - Where Alan builds robots powered by love

⬇️ Basement: Evil Robot Fight Club - Red-lit chaos and destruction

🎯 Your Mission: Deliver 20 passengers to their destinations before chaos erupts!

🎮 How to Play:

1. Wait for game to load (10-20 seconds)

2. CLICK on the game canvas below

3. Press SPACEBAR on your keyboard to start!

⚠️ Must click the game first, then press SPACEBAR ⚠️

🎮 CONTROLS

W or ↑ - Move elevator UP

S or ↓ - Move elevator DOWN

E or SPACE - Open/Close Doors

P - Pause Game

ESC - Quit to Menu

⚡ SPECIAL EVENTS

🌊 Watch out for FLOODS on Floor 4!

💻 Survive the HACKATHON chaos!

🤖 Keep evil robots contained in the basement!
